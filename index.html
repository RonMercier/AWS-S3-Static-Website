About

Engineer. Writer. Builder.

I'm a Cloud & Cybersecurity Engineer based in Raleigh, NC. My career has been built at the intersection of cloud infrastructure and real security work — the kind where the consequences of getting it wrong are immediate and visible.

At Akamai Technologies, I worked on DDoS mitigation and incident response — one of the companies whose literal job is absorbing attacks so other companies stay online. Before that, senior network engineering at AT&T, and hardware engineering at Woven by Toyota.

I'm also a husband, father of five, and someone who genuinely thinks cybersecurity is interesting enough to write about. SecureByDefault.io is where I put real attack breakdowns, honest tool reviews, and security content written for people who don't need it simplified.
